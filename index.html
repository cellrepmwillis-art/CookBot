    You (for protein and calorie goals)

    Typing foods, scanning barcodes and entering calories is free and works offline. Your goal and food log are stored only in this browser. If a scanned product isn't in the built-in list, you can choose to look it up on Open Food Facts, which sends only the barcode number. Photo estimates are optional: they send the photo to the Anthropic API using your own key from console.anthropic.com, which bills a few cents per photo.

    Food data: USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Release 28 (public domain), and Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License. Packaged-product values are entered by Open Food Facts volunteers, so check the label if a number looks off.

    Backup (for a new phone or cleared browser)

    Saves your goal, profile, streak, log and remembered foods to a file. Your API key isn't included.
